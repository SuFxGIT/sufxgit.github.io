What makes it different

The numbers that actually matter

01 · Savings rate and cash flow

See what you keep, and where it is heading.

Savings rate is the share of your income you keep after expenses, tracked over the year. Cash flow is your running total of income minus expenses. Two numbers, one screen, so you know if this month was better than the last.

Trends
02 · Detailed stats

Stats that go five tabs deep.

Overview, Insights, Timeline, Trends and History. Break spending down by category, account or item. See every expense as a share of your income, by day, week, month or year.

Stats
03 · Reminders

A reminder for every bill.

Set a reminder on each expense: an hour, a day, three days or a week before it is due, with your own message. It lands on your lock screen, with no account and no push server involved. The calendar shows everything coming up.

Calendar
04 · Subscriptions and budgets

Recurring bills, under control.

Track subscriptions with their billing cycle, pause or archive them, and set budgets per category or account. EXPN shows spent, remaining and over budget at a glance.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

EXPN works without an account. There is no analytics, no ad tracking, and the app never touches your camera, contacts, location or photos. Cloud sync is optional, and you turn it on with Apple, Google or an email address only if you want your data on more than one device.
