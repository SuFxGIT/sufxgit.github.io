Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026. This policy applies to all SUFX apps: EXPN and Triv14.

The short version

SUFX apps are built to keep your data yours. EXPN works fully without an account and stores your financial data locally on your device. If you buy EXPN Premium and sign in, your data is also stored in a private space tied to your account, so it syncs across your devices and the EXPN website. Triv14 is an online quiz game, so it needs an account and shows a public player profile to other players; the Triv14 section below explains exactly what that involves. There are no ads, no data selling, and no tracking beyond anonymous crash reporting in EXPN.

EXPN

The sections from here to "Triv14" describe EXPN.

Data stored on your device

EXPN keeps everything you enter in the app (expenses, income, budgets, profiles, settings) in a local database on your device. Unless you use cloud sync, this data never leaves your device except in backup files you export yourself. Backups are files you create and keep wherever you choose; SUFX never receives a copy. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and App lock uses your device's own biometrics or passcode; neither sends anything to SUFX.

Sign-in

Signing in is optional and available with Apple, Google, or an email address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, your basic account identifiers (name, if your provider shares it, and email address) are processed to identify your account. If you sign up with email, Firebase sends you a verification email.

Cloud sync (Premium)

With EXPN Premium, when you are signed in, EXPN syncs your app data (expenses, income, budgets, profiles, and settings) to a private space tied to your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so it is available on all your devices. Only your account can access this data. Every request is protected by Firebase security rules and by device attestation (Firebase App Check, which uses Google Play Integrity on Android, Apple App Attest or DeviceCheck on iPhone, and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise on the website). If you never sign in, or do not have Premium, nothing is uploaded.

EXPN on the web (Premium)

Premium includes the EXPN website (expn.web.app). You sign in with the same account, and the website reads and edits your synced data directly in your browser. Your browser keeps only small preferences (language, the profile you last opened) and cached exchange rates; your financial data is not stored in the browser after you close or sign out.

Purchases and Premium

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. SUFX never sees or stores your payment details. To apply Premium to your account on every device, EXPN sends your purchase receipt identifiers to SUFX's server (Firebase Cloud Functions), which checks them with Apple or Google. SUFX stores the purchase's identifier, which account it belongs to, and its status (for example, whether it was refunded or is a test purchase), so Premium follows your account and one purchase cannot be reused by other accounts. New purchases also carry an anonymous account token derived from your account ID, so Apple and Google can tie the purchase to your EXPN account without learning who you are. Apple and Google notify SUFX's server of refunds so Premium can be updated.

Exchange rates

EXPN fetches currency exchange rates from a public dataset (served by the jsDelivr network) over HTTPS. This request contains no personal or financial data.

App updates

EXPN checks Firebase Remote Config for notices about new app versions. This uses a random app installation identifier and contains no personal or financial data.

Crash reports

EXPN uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash and error reports (device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical details of the problem). These reports contain none of your personal or financial information and are used only to find and fix bugs.

Triv14

Triv14 is a live one-on-one trivia game for iPhone and Android. The sections below describe Triv14.

Account and sign-in

Playing Triv14 requires an account. You sign in with Google, or with Apple on iPhone, handled by Firebase Authentication, which keeps your email address to identify your account. Triv14 may suggest your first name as a username when you sign up; it is only saved if you choose to use it.

Your public profile

Other players can see your profile, in duels, on leaderboards and on your profile page. It contains:

  • the username you choose (unique, and changeable once every 30 days)
  • your country, and your city only if you choose one from the list (Triv14 never uses GPS or your device's location)
  • your level, XP, rank, duel statistics, achievements, banners and, with Premium, your chosen badges

Your email address is never shown to other players.

Duels and gameplay data

Duels run on SUFX's server (Firebase Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore), which keeps the questions and answers so nobody can cheat. To score each round it records which answer you picked and how quickly. After a duel, both players can review every question, including what each of you answered. Finished duels are deleted automatically after 3 days; your totals (XP, wins and similar statistics) stay on your profile.

Ghost recordings

When you play a set of questions for the first time, your answers and timings may be kept as a recording. If another player cannot find a live opponent, they may play against that recording, which shows your username, level and country. Recordings are deleted when you delete your account.

Friends and challenges

Each player has a friend code. Friends are added only when both players agree. Friend requests and challenges store the two players involved and are deleted automatically (challenges after a day, unanswered friend requests after 30 days).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Triv14 stores your device's push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and uses it only to tell you about friend requests and challenges. It is deleted with your account.

Question translation

If you choose another language for questions, the question text is translated by Google Cloud Translation and the translation is saved for other players. Only the question text is sent; nothing about you is.

Purchases

Triv14 Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play and handled the same way as EXPN Premium above: SUFX never sees your payment details and stores only the purchase identifier, its status and the account it belongs to.

On your device

Your sound and language settings are stored only on your device.

Where the data is kept

Triv14's server data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the European Union (Belgium).

Children

Triv14 is not directed at children under 13, and SUFX does not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your Triv14 account

In Triv14, open Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, statistics, achievements, friends (including your place on your friends' lists), friend requests, recordings and push token, and frees your username. Duels you were part of are removed within 3 days by the automatic cleanup. You can also email sufxga@gmail.com for help.

What SUFX does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics beyond crash reporting in EXPN.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties. The services named above (Google Firebase, Google Cloud Translation, Apple, Google Play, and jsDelivr) process data only to provide these features.

EXPN data deletion

For deleting Triv14 data, see "Deleting your Triv14 account" above. Uninstalling an app deletes its local data from your device. "Delete all data" in EXPN's settings deletes the data on that device; your cloud copy stays until you delete your account. Deleting your account inside EXPN (Settings, Delete Account) permanently removes your account, your synced cloud data, and your account's Premium record; the purchase record is detached from your account so the purchase can be restored to a new account. You can also email sufxga@gmail.com for help with deletion.
