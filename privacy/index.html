Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026. This policy applies to all SUFX apps.

The short version

SUFX apps are built to keep your data yours. EXPN stores your financial data locally on your device, and SUFX runs no server that receives it. There are no ads, no data selling, and no tracking beyond anonymous crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

EXPN keeps everything you enter in the app (expenses, income, budgets, profiles, settings) in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Backups are files you create and keep wherever you choose; SUFX never receives a copy.

Sign-in

Signing in is optional and available through Apple or Google, handled by Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, only your basic account identifiers (name and email address) are processed, solely to identify your account. Your financial data is not uploaded when you sign in.

Crash reports

SUFX apps use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports (device model, operating system version, and the technical details of the crash). This data contains none of your personal or financial information and is used only to find and fix bugs.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play. SUFX never sees or stores your payment details.

What SUFX does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics beyond crash reporting.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties.

Data deletion

Uninstalling an app deletes its local data from your device. To request deletion of a sign-in account, email sufxga@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any privacy questions, email sufxga@gmail.com.
